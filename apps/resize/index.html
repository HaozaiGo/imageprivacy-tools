What it is for

Resize images for websites, forms, support tickets, marketplaces, social posts, and email attachments without uploading the source file.

Privacy note

Always keep your original privately and share a resized copy when the full resolution is not needed.

Recommended workflow

Start from a private original, choose the smallest dimensions that still show the needed detail, keep the aspect ratio locked, then export a new public copy.

When resizing helps

Resizing is useful when a platform does not need the original resolution, when upload limits are strict, or when you want to reduce unnecessary visual detail.

Before sharing

Open the resized image and check that no addresses, faces, account details, or screen text are still visible. Use the redaction tool first if needed.

FAQ

Does resizing remove EXIF?
Canvas export creates a new image and removes most embedded metadata, but you should still use an EXIF checker for sensitive publishing workflows.
