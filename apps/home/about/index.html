About

Private image tools for safer sharing.

ImagePrivacy collects focused browser tools for removing hidden photo metadata, adding watermarks, redacting screenshots, resizing, compressing, and converting image files before publishing or sharing.

Why it exists

Images often contain hidden metadata, visible private details, or more resolution than a public copy needs. ImagePrivacy makes those cleanup workflows fast, local-first, and easier to review before a file leaves your device.

How it works

The tools are designed to run in your browser whenever possible. The main domain introduces the toolkit, while EXIF, watermark, redaction, resizing, compression, and conversion tools live on dedicated subdomains.

Content focus

ImagePrivacy publishes guides about photo metadata, EXIF cleanup, watermark placement, and safer image sharing workflows. The goal is to help visitors understand both visible image content and hidden file information.

Maintenance and contact

The site is maintained as an independent image privacy toolkit and updated when tools, browser support, or publishing guidance changes. For corrections, broken links, or content questions, contact bb83100436@gmail.com.

Advertising policy

Advertising may appear on the site after review, but ads should remain visually separate from upload areas, download controls, tool results, and examples. The site does not ask visitors to click ads or treat ads as part of a privacy result.

Who it is for

The site is built for creators, sellers, students, small teams, and everyday users who need a simple privacy checklist before posting or sending images. It is not a substitute for legal copyright advice or professional security review.
