About

Private image tools for safer sharing.

ImagePrivacy collects focused browser tools for removing hidden photo metadata and adding image watermarks before publishing or sharing.

Why it exists

Images often contain metadata or need lightweight attribution before they are shared. ImagePrivacy makes those workflows fast and local-first.

How it works

The tools are designed to run in your browser whenever possible. The main domain introduces the toolkit, while EXIF and watermark tools live on dedicated subdomains.

Content focus

ImagePrivacy publishes guides about photo metadata, EXIF cleanup, watermark placement, and safer image sharing workflows. The goal is to help visitors understand both visible image content and hidden file information.

Who it is for

The site is built for creators, sellers, students, small teams, and everyday users who need a simple privacy checklist before posting or sending images. It is not a substitute for legal copyright advice or professional security review.
