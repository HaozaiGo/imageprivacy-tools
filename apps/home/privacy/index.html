Privacy Policy

Image privacy starts with local processing.

Last updated: October 20, 2026. Browser-side image processing is different from a website making no network requests.

Image files and page requests

The image tools are designed to process selected files in your browser, rather than send them to an ImagePrivacy server for editing. That describes the intended file-processing flow, not an independent security certification. Loading pages, code, analytics, and advertising can still contact external servers. Do not use this website for files that your organization requires to remain in an approved offline environment.

Analytics and technical data

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate page usage and site performance. Hosting and analytics providers may receive limited technical information such as the requested page, browser type, device type, approximate region, and request timing. ImagePrivacy does not send selected image files or editing output to analytics services.

Google advertising requests and cookies

The homepage and some guides load Google AdSense code, which can make network requests even if no ad is displayed. This privacy page does not load the advertising code. Where advertising is served, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on those visits. See how Google uses partner-site information. These requests are separate from processing an image file.

Advertising and browser choices

Open Google My Ad Center to manage Google ad personalization. Open AboutAds choices for participating vendors. Browser settings can block or clear cookies and site data. These external choices do not revoke site-specific consent, stop all network requests, or necessarily remove ads.

On pages where Google's consent controls are available, use the footer's “Privacy and cookie settings” control to reopen them. This policy page does not itself load a consent manager. If that control is unavailable, the external choices above remain accessible; report a missing site control through our contact page.

Advertising placement

Advertisements, when shown, should be distinguishable from site navigation, upload controls, download buttons, and image editing actions. We do not ask visitors to click ads, and we do not intentionally place ads where they can be confused with tool controls.

Metadata and visible content

Removing metadata does not automatically remove visible information inside an image, such as faces, addresses, license plates, screenshots, or document text. Visitors are responsible for reviewing the image itself before sharing.

Consent configuration status

Browser storage may be used for local preferences. Publication and regional behavior of a Google-certified consent management platform have not been verified for this site. A link to privacy settings is not evidence of certification or compliant consent handling. Google describes its regional requirements in its publisher consent guidance. Account configuration and live regional verification are still required.

Messages you send us

If you email us, your email provider and ours process your address, message, and any attachments. Contact email is not part of local image processing. Please send a description or a harmless reproduction, not private source files.
