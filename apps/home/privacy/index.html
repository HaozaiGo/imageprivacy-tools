Privacy Policy

Image privacy starts with local processing.

Last updated: May 22, 2026. ImagePrivacy is designed to minimize unnecessary transfer of image files.

Image files

EXIF removal and watermark workflows are designed to run in your browser. We do not intentionally upload or store your selected images as part of the normal tool flow.

Analytics and advertising

We may use analytics and, if enabled, advertising services such as Google AdSense. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Metadata and visible content

Removing metadata does not automatically remove visible information inside an image, such as faces, addresses, license plates, screenshots, or document text. Visitors are responsible for reviewing the image itself before sharing.

Cookies and consent

Essential browser storage may be used for local preferences. Analytics or advertising providers may set cookies, measure visits, or personalize ads where allowed. Visitors can manage cookies through browser settings and any consent controls shown on the site.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact options on the contact page.
