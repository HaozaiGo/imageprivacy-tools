What it is for

Compress images for support tickets, marketplaces, blog posts, email, and pages where the original file is larger than needed.

Privacy note

Compression is not the same as redaction. Check visible private details before sharing.

Recommended workflow

Choose a target width, lower quality gradually, and compare the preview with the original so text, product details, and important edges stay readable.

JPEG or WebP?

JPEG is widely accepted and good for photos. WebP can produce smaller files, but some older systems, forms, or support portals may still expect JPEG.

Before sharing

Compression can make files smaller but it does not hide visible secrets. Redact sensitive areas and remove metadata before sending public copies.

FAQ

Will compression damage quality?
Lower quality settings reduce file size by discarding detail. Use the highest quality that still meets your upload or page-size limit.
